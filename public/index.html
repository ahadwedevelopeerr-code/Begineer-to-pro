A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

meet answers.

Still curious? Ask the AI Tutor anything about web development.

Is CodePath suitable for complete beginners?+

Yes. Start with HTML Foundations and work through the lessons in order. Each lesson introduces one idea, demonstrates it, and gives you a practical exercise.

Do I need to pay or create a student account?+

No student account is needed. Lesson and quiz progress is saved locally in this browser on your device.

What should I learn first?+

Begin with HTML, then CSS, JavaScript, and Node.js. The sequence builds from page structure to styling, browser behavior, and server-side applications.

Can I use the AI Tutor in another language?+

Yes. Ask in English, Urdu, or Roman Urdu and the tutor will try to respond in the language and script you use.
